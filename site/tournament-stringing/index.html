Tournament Stringing: On-Site Stringing Room Setup and Management

URacquet Shop sets up and manages a professional tournament stringing room on site at the location of the tournament — the team, the machines and the player coordination, run by a USRSA Master Racquet Technician.

What we provide

  • On site mobilization and set up of professional stringing room with operation hours based on the needs of the tournament.
  • Management of multi-person stringing team consisting only of tournament experienced master racquet stringers.
  • Set up and usage of multiple Wilson Baiardo stringing machines, the industry standard machine used at pro tournaments such as the US Open and Roland Garros.
  • Racquet drop off/pick up table, stringing machines and stencil station all located in the same location.
  • Direct coordination with players and coaches for an on site racquet pick up schedule based on the needs of each player.
  • “On-Court” rush racquet stringing service available while matches are taking place with immediate delivery to the match court.
  • Player racquet totals and budget tracking available at any time during and after the tournament.
  • Flexible invoicing and payment terms per tournament requirements. Credit/debit card payments accepted.
  • Stringing room tear down and load out procedures per tournament requirements.

Experience

  • WTA 125 Philadelphia Open (2026) — stringing team manager and lead stringer for the inaugural event
  • ITA Northeast Regional Championships — primary stringer and team leader, multiple years
  • ECAC Conference Championships — primary stringer and team leader, three years running

Mark has also strung on the ATP 250 Winston-Salem Open and ATP Columbus Challenger stringing teams and at the Eddie Herr International Junior Championships, and has been the University of Pennsylvania’s varsity stringer since 2022. More background on the About page →
